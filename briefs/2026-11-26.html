2. Google DeepMind launches Mass Spec Skills for Google Antigravity,

Google DeepMind 推出用於 Google Antigravity 的 Mass Spec Skills，

to help identify molecules from tandem mass spectra

協助從串聯質譜辨識分子

Google DeepMind said on 2026-11-25 that it was sharing Mass Spec Skills for Google Antigravity, a suite of domain skills that pair Gemini’s chemical reasoning with established mass-spectrometry tools and public chemical databases.

Google DeepMind 於 2026-11-25 表示，正在推出用於 Google Antigravity 的 Mass Spec Skills，這套領域技能把 Gemini 的化學推理，與既有的質譜工具及公開化學數據庫配對使用。

The company said a modern mass spectrometer can detect tens of thousands of molecular signals in a single sample, but that spectral searches against reference libraries still annotate on average only about 5% of measured spectra, or about 10% for human samples, leaving most signals in what researchers have called the “dark matter” of mass spectrometry.

公司稱，一部現代質譜儀可在單一樣本中偵測數以萬計的分子訊號，但對照參考譜庫的搜尋，平均仍只能為約 5% 的測得圖譜註解，人體樣本則約為 10%，其餘大多數訊號仍屬於研究人員所稱的質譜「暗物質」。

It said the agent can take an unannotated tandem mass spectrum to a proposed structure and an auditable report, laying out the evidence for a chemist to review, and that researchers can describe the task in plain language.

公司表示，該代理可把未註解的串聯質譜，做到提出化學結構及可供審核的報告，並把證據整理供化學家覆核；研究人員亦可用日常語言描述任務。

To test identification and verification, DeepMind said it evaluated the system on 100 compounds sampled from the Enamine REAL MS/MS collection in MoNA, comparing Antigravity with no internet access or skills, with user-permitted internet access, and with that access plus the Mass Spec Skills, across Gemini 3.8 Flash and the frontier models Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 4 Carbon.

為測試辨識及核實能力，DeepMind 表示在 MoNA 中的 Enamine REAL MS/MS 譜集抽取 100 種化合物作評估，並比較三種設定：Antigravity 沒有互聯網或技能、有用戶允許的互聯網存取，以及在該存取之上加上 Mass Spec Skills；測試模型包括 Gemini 3.8 Flash，以及前沿模型 Gemini 4 Argon 與 Gemini 4 Carbon。

With no internet access or skills, it said accuracy remained below 4% across all three models; with the Mass Spec Skills added, all three models identified and verified the exact molecule in around 97% of attempts, and the lightweight model matched the frontier models’ accuracy.

公司稱，在沒有互聯網或技能時，三個模型的準確度都低於 4%；加入 Mass Spec Skills 後，三個模型都在約 97% 的嘗試中辨識並核實了正確分子，輕量模型的準確度亦與前沿模型看齊。

In a worked example in the same post, the agent accounted for 97.7% of the summed fragment intensity, matching 14 of 19 preprocessed peaks, while distinguishing look-alike isomers by testing whether their bond connectivity matched the observed fragmentation pathways.

在同一篇文章的示範例子中，該代理解釋了合計碎片強度的 97.7%，對得上 19 個預處理峰之中的 14 個，並透過檢驗化學鍵連接是否符合觀測到的碎裂路徑，區分外形相近的同分異構體。

https://deepmind.google/blog/interpreting-mass-spectra-with-google-antigravity/

2. USTR determines 14 economies’ excess-capacity practices are actionable,

美國貿易代表認定 14 個經濟體的過剩產能做法可採取行動，

and proposes additional tariffs that are now open for public comment

並提出加徵關稅的建議，現正公開徵詢意見

The Office of the U.S. Trade Representative said on 2026-11-25 that Trade Representative Jamieson Greer had determined under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 that the acts, policies and practices of 14 economies relating to structural excess capacity and production in manufacturing sectors are unreasonable and burden or restrict U.S. commerce, and are thus actionable.

美國貿易代表辦公室於 2026-11-25 表示，貿易代表 Jamieson Greer 已根據 1974 年《貿易法》第 301 條認定，14 個經濟體與製造業結構性過剩產能及生產相關的作為、政策及做法不合理，並對美國商務構成負擔或限制，因此可採取行動。

The 14 economies named in the determinations are China, the European Union, Singapore, Switzerland, Indonesia, Malaysia, Cambodia, Thailand, Korea, Vietnam, Taiwan, Bangladesh, Japan and India; USTR said it has not made determinations with respect to Mexico or Norway at this time, although those two were among the 16 economies originally investigated.

認定所涵蓋的 14 個經濟體為中國、歐盟、新加坡、瑞士、印尼、馬來西亞、柬埔寨、泰國、韓國、越南、台灣、孟加拉、日本及印度；貿易代表辦公室稱，目前尚未對墨西哥或挪威作出認定，儘管這兩地屬於原先受調查的 16 個經濟體之列。

USTR said the proposed actions are additional tariffs, except for certain products identified in the annexes of the respective Federal Register notices, and it described them as proposals for public comment, not as duties already in force.

貿易代表辦公室稱，建議措施是加徵關稅，惟各份《聯邦公報》通知附件所列的若干產品除外；並把這些措施說明為供公眾評論的建議，而非已經實施的關稅。

On the official page, the proposed rates include up to 20% on certain goods of China, up to 35% on goods of Vietnam with a possible adjustment to 20% if Vietnam reaches a responsive reciprocal-trade agreement, up to 25% on goods of India with a possible 17% rate if such an agreement is reached, up to 20% on goods of Singapore with a possible adjustment to 10% if an agreement is reached, 15% on goods of the EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan, net of most-favored-nation rates and existing Section 301 tariffs as described in the notice, and up to 18% on goods of Bangladesh, Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand, in each case with exemptions for certain goods.

在官方頁面中，建議稅率包括：中國若干貨品最高 20%；越南貨品最高 35%，若越南達成具回應性的對等貿易協議則可能調整為 20%；印度貨品最高 25%，若達成協議則可能為 17%；新加坡貨品最高 20%，若達成協議則可能調整為 10%；歐盟、日本、韓國、瑞士及台灣貨品為 15%，並按通知所述扣除最惠國稅率及既有第 301 條關稅；孟加拉、柬埔寨、印尼、馬來西亞及泰國貨品最高 18%；以上各項均有若干貨品豁免。

The notice said interested persons should submit requests to appear at the hearings, along with a summary of testimony, by January 5, 2027, that written comments are due by the same date, and that USTR will hold hearings on the proposed actions on January 25, 2027, continuing as appropriate.

通知稱，有意出席聽證會的人士須在 2027 年 1 月 5 日或之前提交出席申請及證詞摘要，書面意見亦須於同一日前遞交；貿易代表辦公室將於 2027 年 1 月 25 日就建議措施舉行聽證會，並視乎情況延續。
