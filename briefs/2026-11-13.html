Google 推出 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，取代 3.8 Flash 成為高流量編碼與代理工作的新預設

Google 週四由 DeepMind 高級副總裁兼首席人工智能架構師 Koray Kavukcuoglu 宣布 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，定位為面向日常編碼與代理工作流程的快速、低成本執行模型，並在整個 100 萬 token 上下文窗口以每百萬 input token $1.50、output token $7.50 劃一定價。官方稱其預設 high 思考檔在 Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 錄得 51，高於 Argon 的 50，xhigh 檔可達 54；相對 3.8 Flash，預設檔完成任務速度快 2.5 倍，Terminal-Bench 4.1 由 18.2% 升至 57%。Hydrogen 即日起在 Gemini API、AI Studio、Gemini 應用程式及 Google 搜尋等面向全球推出，免費用戶可經自動型號選擇使用；Google 並在 Antigravity 引入 Orchestrator，可在同一會話中動態切換 Carbon 與 Hydrogen。

Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen as its new high-volume default for coding and agent work, succeeding 3.8 Flash

Google on Thursday, in a post by DeepMind SVP and chief AI architect Koray Kavukcuoglu, introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast, lower-cost execution model for everyday coding and agentic workflows, priced flat at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1-million-token context window. Google said Hydrogen scored 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default high thinking level, versus 50 for Argon, and 54 at its new xhigh setting. Versus 3.8 Flash, it said Hydrogen finishes tasks 2.5 times faster at the default setting and more than triples Terminal-Bench 4.1 (57% vs. 18.2%). Hydrogen is rolling out globally in the Gemini API, AI Studio, the Gemini app and Google Search, with free-app access via auto model selection; Google also introduced Orchestrator in Antigravity to switch between Carbon and Hydrogen in the same session.

來源／Source: Google Blog — https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/

比特幣跌至約 12 週低位，低見約 $71,200；CoinGlass 錄得逾 $7.50 億倉位被清算

The Block 報道，比特幣週四跌至近 12 週最低，盤中低見約 $71,200，並在報道時跌 3.4% 報約 $71,600，市場同時消化 Nvidia 行政總裁黃仁勳突然辭任。報道引述 CoinGlass，過去 24 小時有逾 $7.50 億加密倉位被清算，其中多頭超過 $6.34 億。最新 CoinGecko 快照見行情一節。此為風險資產情緒轉弱下的市場反應，而非單一項目的基本面公告。

Bitcoin falls to a roughly 12-week low, trading as low as about $71,200; CoinGlass records more than $750 million of liquidations

The Block reported that bitcoin fell to its lowest level in nearly 12 weeks on Thursday, trading as low as about $71,200 and down 3.4% at about $71,600 at the time of publication, as markets digested Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s abrupt resignation. Citing CoinGlass, it said more than $750 million of crypto positions were liquidated over 24 hours, including more than $634 million in longs. The latest CoinGecko snapshot is in the price section below. This was a risk-off market move rather than a single-protocol announcement.

美國現貨比特幣 ETF 週三再錄約 $2.999 億淨流出，連續第五日、五日累計約 $14.4 億

CoinDesk 引述 SoSoValue，美國現貨比特幣 ETF 週三淨流出 $2.999 億，連續第五個交易日錄得資金淨流出，自 11 月 5 日以來約合 $14.4 億；其中貝萊德 IBIT 佔 $2.697 億，為連續第九個交易日贖回。這較本簡報昨日所錄、週二約 $9,500 萬的淨流出明顯擴大，顯示機構需求仍然偏弱。

U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $299.9 million on Wednesday, a fifth straight day and about $1.44 billion over five sessions

CoinDesk, citing SoSoValue, said U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost $299.9 million on Wednesday, a fifth consecutive session of net outflows totaling about $1.44 billion since Nov. 5. BlackRock’s IBIT accounted for $269.7 million, its ninth straight session of redemptions. That was a clear step-up from the about $95 million of Tuesday outflows recorded in yesterday’s brief, and still pointed to soft institutional demand.

3.

MSCI 確認將於 11 月 30 日把 Strategy 移出其指數，市場憂慮被動賣盤

The Block 及 CoinDesk 報道，MSCI 已確認將於 11 月 30 日把比特幣財政儲備公司 Strategy（原 MicroStrategy，代號 MSTR）移出其指數，市場關注這可能引發被動型基金賣出這隻被視為比特幣代理的股份。Wintermute 稱此為「比特幣代理的被動賣盤風險」，但表示沒有規模估算。此為指數編製決定，並非 Strategy 本身公布新的買幣或財務結果。

MSCI confirms it will remove Strategy from its indexes on Nov. 30, raising the prospect of passive selling

The Block and CoinDesk reported that MSCI has confirmed it will remove bitcoin treasury firm Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy, ticker MSTR) from its indexes on Nov. 30, raising the prospect of selling by passive funds in a stock widely treated as a bitcoin proxy. Wintermute called it “a passive-selling risk on a BTC proxy” but said it had no size estimate. This is an index-methodology decision, not a new share-purchase or results announcement from Strategy itself.

4.

紐約州金融服務局就虛擬貨幣廣告及推廣發出新指引，機構須於 2027 年 5 月 10 日前落實

紐約州金融服務局（NYDFS）署理局長 Kaitlin Asrow 週四發布指引，要求受該局發牌或特許的虛擬貨幣機構，就廣告及市場推廣制定並執行全面政策，重點是內容準確、披露清晰，以及建立管治與審批程序。機構須在 2027 年 5 月 10 日前按指引更新政策及程序，其後至少每年檢討，並保存紀錄供該局審查。指引要求避免虛假、誤導或誇大內容，披露須清晰易明並標明提供產品的持牌機構，同時對第三方推廣人做盡職審查，以及監察盜用品牌的行為。

New York DFS issues new advertising and marketing guidance for virtual-currency firms, with a May 10, 2027 implementation date

New York State Department of Financial Services Acting Superintendent Kaitlin Asrow on Thursday issued guidance requiring DFS-licensed or chartered virtual-currency entities to maintain comprehensive advertising and marketing policies, emphasising accurate content, clear disclosures and a governance and review process. Firms are expected to update their policies by May 10, 2027, review them at least annually and keep records for DFS review. The guidance says claims should not be false, misleading or exaggerated; disclosures should be clear and identify the licensed entity; and firms should conduct due diligence on third-party promoters and monitor misuse of their brand.

來源／Source: New York State Department of Financial Services — https://www.dfs.ny.gov/reports_and_publications/press_releases/pr20261112

泰國證監會建議對穩定幣存入及提取設每日 500 萬銖（約 $148,000）上限

Cointelegraph 引述泰國證券交易委員會週四公告，建議受規管加密營運商對每名客戶的穩定幣存入及提取，分別設每日 500 萬銖（約 $148,000）上限，旨在遏止清洗黑錢、網絡罪行及規避跨境匯款規則。草案亦禁止與第三者錢包進行穩定幣轉帳，營運商須核實客戶擁有所用帳戶或錢包，並遵守 Travel Rule 及使用區塊鏈分析。交易所須披露做市商及所涵蓋資產；經紀不得就穩定幣兌泰銖使用流動性供應商；部分場外交易設 300 萬銖最低額。此為諮詢後的草案，並非已生效規則。

Thailand’s SEC proposes a 5 million baht (about $148,000) daily cap on stablecoin deposits and withdrawals

Cointelegraph, citing a Thursday announcement from Thailand’s Securities and Exchange Commission, said the regulator has proposed capping daily stablecoin deposits and withdrawals at 5 million baht (about $148,000) each per customer at regulated crypto operators, to curb money laundering, cybercrime and attempts to bypass cross-border transfer rules. The draft would bar stablecoin transfers to and from third-party wallets, require operators to verify that customers own the accounts or wallets they use, and require Travel Rule compliance and blockchain analytics. Exchanges would have to name market makers and the assets they cover; brokers would be barred from using liquidity providers for stablecoin-baht pairs; and some off-platform trades would face a 3 million baht minimum. This is a draft after consultation, not a rule already in force.

來源／Source: Cointelegraph — https://cointelegraph.com/news/thailand-tighter-stablecoin-rules-crypto-exchange

Visa 與 Anchorage Digital 合作，先以西聯 USDPT 穩定幣作為 Visa 網絡結算資產

Anchorage Digital 週四宣布，與 Visa 合作，讓該行發行的選定穩定幣可作為 Visa 網絡的原生結算資產，首個是西聯的 USDPT。Visa 及合資格夥伴此後可選擇以 USDPT 結算 VisaNet 義務；雙方稱這套安排亦可作為其後納入更多 Anchorage 發行穩定幣的框架。Anchorage 稱 USDPT 等由其發行的穩定幣以美元及等價現金全額支持，可 1:1 贖回，並在聯邦監管下鑄造。這是機構結算基礎設施的擴展，並非新的零售穩定幣發行。

Visa and Anchorage Digital add Western Union’s USDPT stablecoin as a native settlement asset on the Visa network

Anchorage Digital said Thursday it is working with Visa to support selected stablecoins issued by Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A., as native settlement assets on the Visa network, starting with Western Union’s USDPT. Visa and eligible partners can now use USDPT to settle VisaNet obligations, and the firms said the arrangement creates a framework to consider additional Anchorage-issued stablecoins later. Anchorage said the stablecoins it issues, including USDPT, are fully backed by U.S. dollars and cash equivalents, redeemable 1:1, and minted under federal oversight. This is an expansion of institutional settlement infrastructure, not a new retail stablecoin launch.

來源／Source: Anchorage Digital — https://www.anchorage.com/press-room/anchorage-digital-to-support-visa-stablecoin-settlement-starting-with-usdpt
