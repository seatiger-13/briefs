1. OpenAI says GPT-6 models can design new protein structures and sequences,

OpenAI 稱 GPT-6 模型可設計新的蛋白質結構及序列，

and lab tests with Onava found binding across five targets

與 Onava 進行的實驗室測試，更在五個靶點上錄得結合證據

OpenAI said on 2026-11-24 that its GPT-6 family of models can design new protein structures and amino-acid sequences using only reasoning and general-purpose coding tools, without calling specialized biology models to generate those designs.

OpenAI 於 2026-11-24 表示，其 GPT-6 系列模型只需推理及通用編程工具，便可設計新的蛋白質結構及氨基酸序列，而無須呼叫專門的生物學模型來生成這些設計。

In a preprint titled “GPT-6 as a Universal Foundation Model for Protein Biology: Beyond Specialized Models,” the company said an internal research model matched or outperformed specialized models on many of the protein-design tasks it tested, although predicting a complete protein structure remained a clear weakness.

公司在題為《GPT-6 as a Universal Foundation Model for Protein Biology: Beyond Specialized Models》的預印本中表示，一個內部研究模型在許多受測的蛋白質設計任務上，表現與專門模型相若或更佳；但預測完整蛋白質結構仍是明顯弱項。

OpenAI said researchers could guide the designs in natural language, including where a protein should attach and what shape it should take.

OpenAI 稱，研究人員可用自然語言引導設計，包括蛋白質應在何處結合，以及應採取何種形狀。

In laboratory tests with research partner Onava, the company said it tested 322 minibinders against five targets, and that 35 of those designs, or 10.9%, tested positive for binding to their intended target.

在與研究夥伴 Onava 進行的實驗室測試中，公司表示共測試了 322 種小型結合蛋白（minibinder），針對五個靶點；其中 35 個設計（佔 10.9%）對預定靶點呈結合陽性。

It reported five hits for PD-L1, three for IL-7Rα, one for TrkA, eight for VEGF-A and 18 for IL-17A, and said redesigned antibodies also retained binding; specialized models helped supply sequences and select candidates for testing.

公司公布的命中數目為：PD-L1 五個、IL-7Rα 三個、TrkA 一個、VEGF-A 八個、IL-17A 18 個，並稱經重新設計的抗體亦保留結合能力；專門模型有助提供序列及篩選供測試的候選設計。

OpenAI described the results as early evidence that GPT-6.2’s three-dimensional designs can be turned into proteins that bind their intended targets in the lab, and noted that reported hit rates depend heavily on how strictly designs are filtered before validation and on the definition of binding.

OpenAI 將結果視為早期證據，顯示 GPT-6.2 的三維設計可製成能在實驗室與其預定靶點結合的蛋白質；並指出，已公布的命中率很大程度取決於驗證前如何嚴格篩選設計，以及如何定義「結合」。

2. Google DeepMind launches Humanity’s First Exam,

Google DeepMind 推出 Humanity’s First Exam，

a Kaggle benchmark that tests whether models reliably answer everyday questions

一個在 Kaggle 上測試模型能否可靠回答日常問題的基準

Google DeepMind said on 2026-11-24 that it was launching the Humanity’s First Exam (HFE) benchmark on Kaggle to measure how reliably large language models answer straightforward, everyday questions.

Google DeepMind 於 2026-11-24 表示，正在 Kaggle 推出 Humanity’s First Exam（HFE）基準，用以衡量大型語言模型回答簡單日常問題時有多可靠。

The company said most current benchmarks measure domain-specific capability ceilings in areas such as complex math, coding and science, whereas HFE asks whether models can consistently solve tasks, and slight variations of those tasks, every time.

該公司表示，現時大多數基準衡量的是複雜數學、編程及科學等領域的能力上限；HFE 則檢視模型是否每次都能穩定地完成同一類任務，以及這些任務的輕微變體。

It said the dataset consists of 544 elementary text and image prompts across 20 foundational capabilities, and that frontier models have made significant progress on HFE over the last six months.

公司稱數據集包含 544 道基礎文字及圖像提示，涵蓋 20 項基礎能力，並指前沿模型在過去六個月於 HFE 上已有顯著進展。

DeepMind is also running a three-week Kaggle competition through December 15, inviting the community to submit everyday questions that today’s models still get wrong, in order to keep the benchmark up to date.

DeepMind 亦舉辦為期三週、至 12 月 15 日截止的 Kaggle 比賽，邀請社群提交現時模型仍然答錯的日常問題，以保持基準更新。

公司稱，新的預覽功能把 Insights 接駁至 Agent Optimizer，並支援人工標註及評分準則評估器，亦包括 Programmed Training，即由團隊編寫訓練迴圈，再由 Foundry 運行 GPU。

Microsoft 表示，Agent Optimizer 絕不會自動更改生產環境中的智能體。
