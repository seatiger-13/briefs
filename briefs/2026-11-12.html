1.

DeepMind 推出 AlphaGenome 2，預測基因變異如何影響特定人類細胞類型

Google DeepMind 週三宣布 AlphaGenome 2，把預測範圍由組織平均值推進至特定細胞類型與細胞狀態，並稱在 TenK10K 單細胞數據上，識別具因果作用的基因表現變異數量接近上一代的兩倍（約 30% 對 16%）。模型以超過 1 億個單細胞圖譜、涵蓋 68 種組織訓練，現已透過 AlphaGenome API 供非商業研究使用，商業研究則為私人預覽；官方強調僅供研究、尚未通過臨床或診斷用途驗證。

DeepMind launches AlphaGenome 2 to predict how DNA variants affect specific human cell types

Google DeepMind on Wednesday announced AlphaGenome 2, which conditions predictions on a specific cell type or state rather than broad tissue averages. It said the model identified nearly twice as many causal gene-expression variants on the TenK10K dataset (about 30% versus 16%). Trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues, it is available today for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API and in private preview for commercial research; DeepMind said it is for research use only and has not been validated for clinical or diagnostic purposes.

來源／Source: Google DeepMind — https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/

Google 推出 Gemini Decisions API 與 Decision 1，一次可評估最多 1,000 道封閉式問題

Google 週三推出 Gemini Decisions API，由新模型 Gemini Decision 1 驅動，可在單次前向傳遞中，對文字、圖像、音訊、視訊或 PDF（最長約 25.6 萬 token）同時處理最多 1,000 道是非、選擇或量尺評分問題，並回傳機率與置信度。官方稱端到端延遲約 150–250 毫秒，定價為每百萬 input token $0.10、不收取 output token；Google 指內部已用於 Gemini Notebook 的模型路由、Stitch 畫布及搜尋的工具安全層。

Google launches the Gemini Decisions API and Decision 1 to score up to 1,000 closed-set questions at once

Google on Wednesday introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by Gemini Decision 1, which scores up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice or rubric questions in a single pass over a shared multimodal input (text, images, audio, video or PDFs, up to 256K tokens) and returns probabilities and confidence scores. Google put end-to-end latency at about 150–250 milliseconds and priced the model at $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge. It said Google teams already use it for model routing in Gemini Notebook, rapid canvases in Stitch, and a safety layer for tools in Search.

來源／Source: Google Blog — https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/

AWS 發布完全由自主 AI 代理編寫的本機模擬器 AWS Emulator

Amazon 週三推出可本機運行的 AWS Emulator，供開發者在不上雲的情況下建置及測試應用，並稱全部程式碼由數千個自主 AI 代理編寫，團隊沒有撰寫或審閱任何程式碼。系統以測試套件、找問題與修補三組代理協作，變更須通過編譯、安全與保真度門檻才會合併；原始碼以 CC0 授權公開於 GitHub，並持續更新。

AWS releases AWS Emulator, a local emulator written entirely by autonomous AI agents

Amazon on Wednesday released AWS Emulator, a downloadable local emulator of AWS services for building and testing applications without calling the cloud. It said thousands of autonomous agents wrote every line of code and that the team had not written or reviewed any of it. Three agent teams manage tests, find issues and fix them, with merges gated on compile, security and fidelity checks. The source is on GitHub under a CC0 license and is being updated continuously.

來源／Source: About Amazon / AWS — https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/how-autonomous-ai-agents-built-aws-emulator

Runway 以 Apache 2.0 開源開放 Praxis-1 機器人世界動作模型權重

Runway 週三向所有開發者與研究人員開放 270 億參數的 Praxis-1，並以 Apache 2.0 授權提供權重及微調程式碼（含商業使用）。公司稱該模型在 NVIDIA RoboLab-120 上 1,200 次試驗的成功率為 46.2%，高於所列其他開放權重視覺—語言—動作模型；Noble Machines、Standard Bots 與 Ultra 等合作夥伴已在人形、工業機械臂及倉儲機器人上運行。模型可於 GitHub、Hugging Face 及 vLLM-Omni 取得。

Runway open-weights Praxis-1, its robotics world-action model, under Apache 2.0

Runway on Wednesday made its 27-billion-parameter Praxis-1 world-action model available to all developers and researchers, with weights and fine-tuning code under Apache 2.0, including for commercial use. It said Praxis-1 led NVIDIA’s RoboLab-120 leaderboard at 46.2% success across 1,200 episodes, ahead of the other open-weight VLA models it listed. Launch partners including Noble Machines, Standard Bots and Ultra have been running it on a humanoid, an industrial arm and a warehouse robot. It is available on GitHub, Hugging Face and in vLLM-Omni.

來源／Source: Runway — https://runway.com/news/company-news/praxis-1-is-now-available

MiniMax 開源發布 H3-Image，稱可在 10 秒內生成 4K 圖像

MiniMax 週三發布開放權重圖像生成與編輯模型 H3-Image，支援文字生成圖像、圖像編輯，以及最多 16 張參考圖，並稱可在 10 秒內生成 4096×4096（4K）圖像，速度可達其所述主流方案的約 5 倍。權重已上載 Hugging Face，同時亦可經 MiniMax API、MiniMax Code 與 Hailuo AI 使用；官方並為上線週提供 4K 或以上解析度五折優惠。

MiniMax releases open-weight H3-Image, saying it can generate 4K images in under 10 seconds

MiniMax on Wednesday released H3-Image, an open-weight image generation and editing model that supports text-to-image, editing and up to 16 reference images. It said the model can produce a 4096×4096 (4K) image in under 10 seconds, up to about 5× the speed of the mainstream alternatives it cited. Weights are on Hugging Face, and the model is also available through the MiniMax API, MiniMax Code and Hailuo AI, with 50% off 4K-and-above requests for launch week.

來源／Source: MiniMax — https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3-image

DeepL unveils Aure, a voice-translation model that follows tone and pacing in real time

DeepL on Wednesday introduced Aure, its latest Voice AI model for DeepL Voice real-time translation. The capabilities apply automatically across DeepL Voice for online meetings, in-person conversations and the Voice API, initially in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Korean.

NEAR AI 推出 Gateway，讓 Claude Code、Codex 與 OpenCode 接駁私有模型

NEAR AI 週三推出 macOS 應用 Gateway，可把 Claude Code、Codex 與 OpenCode 連到其私有及「隱身」（incognito）模型，讓開發者在常用工具裡處理敏感工作。官方稱私有模型在安全硬體飛地中推論；隱身模式則經 NEAR AI Cloud 憑證轉接外部供應商，供應商仍會處理請求。用戶可在本機檢視工作階段追蹤數據，並可選擇在審批後分享經遮敏的數據以換取雲端額度。

NEAR AI launches Gateway to connect Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode to private models

NEAR AI on Wednesday introduced a macOS app, Gateway, that launches Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode sessions already configured for its private and incognito models, so developers can keep sensitive work inside familiar tools. It said private models run inference in secure hardware enclaves, while incognito models reach external providers through NEAR AI Cloud credentials and those providers still process the requests. Users can inspect session traces on their own machine and, if they opt in, share redacted traces they approve in exchange for cloud credits.

EBA direct supervision of EU-issued USDC takes effect on Nov. 12, as previously scheduled
