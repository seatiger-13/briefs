1. OpenAI launches a science blog and publishes a candidate lattice definition of the Standard Model developed with GPT-6 Astra

OpenAI 週日開設科學博客，並刊出物理學家與 GPT-6 Astra 合作的標準模型格點定義候選方案

OpenAI said on November 15 that it was starting a science blog for scientists and academic researchers to share discoveries, methods and perspectives on work done with AI, including contributions from OpenAI, its collaborators and the broader scientific community. The first guest post, published the same day, is by Nathaniel Craig, who leads the particle theory group at the University of California, Santa Barbara and is a visiting scientist at OpenAI. Craig describes working with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model. He wrote that he had a strategy but had not been able to prove it in all the cases it needed to cover, and that Astra helped turn the strategy into a construction and took the lead in developing the detailed proof. The post presents the work as a candidate and says questions remain, including defining the interacting theory in continuous spacetime; Craig also pointed to OpenAI’s recent proof of the existence of Yang-Mills theory as a missing piece. An October preprint had described the proposal; this item records Sunday’s public presentation, not the earlier preprint as if it were new today.

OpenAI 於 11 月 15 日宣布開設科學博客，供該公司、合作者及更廣泛科研社群分享以人工智能進行的科學發現、方法與觀點。同日刊出首篇特約文章，加州大學聖塔芭芭拉分校粒子理論組負責人、訪問科學家 Nathaniel Craig 記述他與 GPT-6 Astra 合作，就標準模型提出一個不依賴微擾展開的格點定義候選方案。Craig 稱他原有策略，但未能在所有必要情況完成證明；Astra 協助把策略寫成構造，並在詳細證明中起主導作用。文章指這仍是候選方案，連續時空中的相互作用理論等問題仍待解決；他並提到 OpenAI 近期有關楊－米爾斯理論存在性的證明，為此提供了一塊拼圖。相關結果先前已有 10 月預印本，本則記錄週日的正式公開闡述，並非把 10 月預印本當成今日新發現。
