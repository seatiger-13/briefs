1. OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet, requiring a biometric check on every agent purchase; U.S. Pro users first

OpenAI 推出 ChatGPT Wallet：代理購物須逐筆生物識別核准，先向美國 Pro 用戶開放

OpenAI on November 13 introduced ChatGPT Wallet, which it called the first wallet that lets agents pay without sharing users’ actual card details with the business or the agent, and that requires a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint for every purchase, while preserving existing card rewards and eligible dispute protections. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it is rolling out first in dots to ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older in the U.S. with eligible U.S.-issued cards. Users add a card once, review the items, total and selected card, then approve; the network then issues a payment token. OpenAI also published a Commerce Partner Playbook to help businesses avoid inadvertently blocking agent purchases.

OpenAI 於 11 月 13 日宣布推出 ChatGPT Wallet，稱這是首個讓代理付款、但不向商家或代理分享用戶真實卡號，並要求每筆購物都通過 Face ID 或指紋等保安核對的錢包，同時保留合資格卡的獎賞及爭議保障。產品與 American Express、Mastercard 及 Visa 合作建立，先在 dots 向 18 歲或以上、持合資格美國發卡的 ChatGPT Pro 訂戶推出。用戶先綁定一張卡，每筆交易先核對商品、總額及所用卡，核准後支付網絡會發出代用付款代幣；OpenAI 並發布 Commerce Partner Playbook，協助商家避免其網站或風控系統誤擋代理購物。

2. Google DeepMind opens SatWeave and PARDIG, the datasets behind its AI weather models

Google DeepMind 開放 WeatherNext 背後數據集 SatWeave 與 PARDIG

Google DeepMind’s WeatherNext team on November 13 released SatWeave and PARDIG, two foundational datasets it said were key to its recently released WeatherNext 3 model, with historical archives and live feeds now available on Google Cloud Storage and PARDIG’s historical catalogs also on Google Earth Engine. SatWeave is a global mosaic of geostationary satellite imagery across 17 visible, water-vapor and infrared channels, updated hourly. PARDIG (Precipitation AI Reanalysis: Densely Inferred GPM) is trained on sparse spaceborne radar from NASA’s Global Precipitation Measurement Core Observatory, using SatWeave imagery and ECMWF atmospheric fields, and produces a global precipitation map at about 15-minute and roughly 10-kilometer resolution, with average latency of about five hours. DeepMind said evaluations against ground radar networks in the U.S., Europe and Japan from 2016 to 2023 showed PARDIG improving hourly precipitation detection skill over NASA’s IMERG-Final by more than 30% on light-to-moderate rain, and that it was particularly effective at capturing extreme precipitation.

Google DeepMind 天氣團隊於 11 月 13 日宣布，向研究及開發者開放 SatWeave 與 PARDIG 兩個基礎數據集，兩者都是近日發布的 WeatherNext 3 模型的關鍵輸入，歷史檔案及即時饋送已在 Google Cloud Storage 提供，PARDIG 歷史目錄亦上載 Google Earth Engine。SatWeave 把多顆地球靜止衛星影像整合成涵蓋 17 個可見光、水汽及紅外通道的全球拼接圖，每小時更新；PARDIG（Precipitation AI Reanalysis: Densely Inferred GPM）以 NASA 全球降水測量核心觀測衛星的稀疏星載雷達數據訓練，並結合 SatWeave 及歐洲中期天氣預報中心大氣場，提供約 15 分鐘、約 10 公里解析度的全球降水圖，平均延遲約 5 小時。官方稱對 2016 至 2023 年美、歐、日地面雷達網絡的評估顯示，PARDIG 在輕至中等降水的每小時偵測技巧較 NASA IMERG-Final 高出 30% 以上，並較能捕捉極端降水。

https://deepmind.google/blog/opening-the-datasets-behind-our-ai-weather-models/

3. Google Cloud adds enterprise AI-agent observability across BigQuery and Looker

Google Cloud 在 BigQuery 與 Looker 推出企業級人工智能代理可觀測性

Google Cloud on November 13 announced expanded AI-agent observability and evaluation across its Data Cloud, giving enterprises unified visibility into first-party data agents in BigQuery and Looker as well as custom third-party agents built with frameworks such as the Agent Development Kit or LangGraph. Telemetry lands in customer-owned BigQuery tables and can include Cloud Monitoring metrics, Cloud Trace spans, conversation logs, and token and latency tracking, with prebuilt dashboards in BigQuery Studio and Looker. The launch also adds query spend caps, daily token quotas in preview, and evaluation through the open-source Prism tool and native BigQuery evaluations in preview, so teams can test agents before prompt or model changes reach users.

Google Cloud 於 11 月 13 日宣布，在 Data Cloud 擴大人工智能代理的可觀測及評估能力，讓企業以同一套框架追蹤 BigQuery、Looker 的第一方數據代理，以及用 Agent Development Kit 或 LangGraph 等框架自建的第三方代理。遙測會寫入客戶自有的 BigQuery 表，並可配 Cloud Monitoring 指標、Cloud Trace 跨度、對話日誌、代幣及延遲追蹤，以及 BigQuery Studio 與 Looker 的預建儀表板。新能力亦包括查詢掃描上限、預覽中的每日代幣配額，以及用開源工具 Prism 和原生 BigQuery 評估（預覽）在變更提示或模型前測試代理表現。

3. CFTC Chair Selig says major exchanges and new entrants are looking to opt into the agency’s crypto rules

In a Friday CNBC interview, CFTC Chairman Michael Selig said major crypto exchanges and new entrants are showing interest in the agency’s proposed federal framework for digital assets. He said many large exchanges are already registered with the CFTC because they offer derivatives such as perpetual futures and would now be able to add spot trading, while new entrants would rather obtain one CFTC license than navigate 50 state regimes. After the Senate failed to advance the Clarity Act in September, the CFTC and SEC moved to build frameworks under existing statutory authority; the oversight is opt-in for exchanges that do not currently offer margined, leveraged or financed trading. Selig also said the agency would fight state lawsuits that treat regulated prediction-market exchanges as unlicensed gambling “up to the Supreme Court if that’s where it has to go.” The rules could face closer scrutiny in 2027, when Democrats are expected to control Congress.
