1. OpenAI signs agreements with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire for its first phase of embedded safety assessments

OpenAI 與 Apollo Research、METR、Transluce 及 Goodfire 簽署協議，展開首階段嵌入式獨立安全評估

OpenAI said on November 16 that it had signed agreements with four independent assessors for its first phase of embedded assessments, giving outside experts deep access over an extended period to internal models and systems so they can evaluate alignment and the effectiveness of safeguards, especially where those bear on the company’s safety cases. Depending on what each evaluation needs, assessors may receive access to some of the same internal resources OpenAI researchers use, including model checkpoints, source code, training and evaluation data, monitoring systems, technical documentation, and discussions with the researchers and engineers responsible for the work. The initial assessments focus on alignment and safeguards during training and internal use. Apollo Research will assess whether models could covertly work against their developers’ intentions and whether safeguards would detect that behavior; Goodfire will embed interpretability researchers to monitor training runs and feed findings back to OpenAI’s own teams; METR plans to assess misaligned behavior in OpenAI’s largest reinforcement-learning run to date and has preliminarily agreed to a broader monitor assessment; and Transluce will examine the quality and coverage of post-training alignment evaluations, with a follow-on assessment of about eight weeks intended for early 2027. Assessors keep independence in publishing, but OpenAI retains the right to redact confidential information and may request responsible-disclosure delays. The company said the work also supports its commitments under the White House Accord on Super Intelligence to independent assessments of its safety controls.

OpenAI 於 11 月 16 日宣布，已與四家獨立評估機構簽署協議，展開首階段嵌入式評估（embedded assessments），讓外部專家在較長時間內深入取用公司內部模型與系統，評估模型對齊及安全措施是否有效，尤其是與其安全論證（safety cases）相關的部分。按各項評估需要，評估人員可取用部分與內部研究人員相同的資源，包括模型檢查點、原始碼、訓練及評估數據、監察系統、技術文件，以及與相關研究人員和工程師討論。首階段聚焦訓練期間及內部使用的對齊與安全措施。Apollo Research 將評估模型會否暗中違背開發者意圖，以及現有措施能否偵測此類行為；Goodfire 將派駐可解釋性研究人員，監察訓練過程並向內部團隊提供改進建議；METR 計劃評估該公司迄今最大規模強化學習訓練中的錯位行為，並另行商討更廣泛的監察評估；Transluce 將檢視訓練後對齊評估的質素與覆蓋範圍，雙方並有意在 2027 年初進行約八週的後續評估。評估人員對公開報告保持獨立，但 OpenAI 保留遮蓋機密資料的權利，亦可要求作負責任披露的延遲。公司稱此舉亦配合其在白宮超級智能協議（White House Accord on Super Intelligence）下接受獨立安全評估的承諾。

2. OpenAI publishes evaluations of computer-use agents for healthcare and details work with CVS Health, Cigna, Abridge and Ambience

OpenAI 公布醫療電腦操作代理評估結果，並與 CVS Health、Cigna、Abridge 及 Ambience 合作應用於授權、轉介及出院等流程

OpenAI said on November 16 that it was sharing new results on the performance, safety and cost of computer-use agents in clinical and administrative workflows, and describing work with Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare and CVS Health on authorization requests, referrals, discharge planning and outreach between visits. Computer-use agents carry out multi-step workflows by clicking, typing and moving between existing applications. OpenAI said that on electronic-health-record inbox tasks, GPT-6 Astra was rated clinically safe in 99% of 325 reviewed tasks, and physicians rated the work “very helpful” or “extremely helpful” in 93% of 646 assessments. On HealthAdminBench, an independent Stanford benchmark of common administrative workflows, GPT-6 Astra earned 94.3% of available rubric points and GPT-6 Luna 91.8%, with Luna costing 8.5 cents per task, 98% less. CVS Health said an agent had cleared a months-long document-sending backlog and that another handles system steps for tens of thousands of consults a day; Cigna Healthcare said case managers previously had to navigate more than 10 systems to gather member context. The capabilities are available through the OpenAI API, ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians, with support for HIPAA-compliant workflows, approval settings and audit logs; organization data is not used to train OpenAI foundation models.

OpenAI 於 11 月 16 日公布，就其電腦操作代理在臨床及行政流程中的表現、安全與成本提出新評估結果，並說明正與 Abridge、Ambience、Cigna Healthcare 及 CVS Health 等機構合作，把有關能力用於事前授權、轉介、出院計劃及診後跟進。電腦操作代理透過點擊、輸入及在現有應用程式之間切換，執行多步工作流程。公司稱，以 GPT-6 Astra 處理電子病歷收件箱任務時，在 325 項經審視任務中有 99% 被評為臨床安全；在 646 次醫師評分中，有 93% 被評為「非常有幫助」或「極有幫助」。在斯坦福研究人員的 HealthAdminBench 行政流程基準上，GPT-6 Astra 取得 94.3% 的評分點，GPT-6 Luna 取得 91.8%，而後者每項任務成本僅 8.5 美仙，便宜 98%。CVS Health 稱已用有關能力清理積壓多月的文件傳送工作，並處理每日數以萬計會診的系統步驟；Cigna Healthcare 稱個案經理以往須查閱逾 10 套系統，現可更快彙集成員背景。相關能力已可經 OpenAI API、ChatGPT for Healthcare 及 ChatGPT for Clinicians 使用，並支援符合 HIPAA 的工作流程、審批設定及審計紀錄；組織數據不會用來訓練 OpenAI 基礎模型。

3. OpenAI expands ChatGPT for Academic Researchers, aiming to reach 200,000 researchers, with 50,000 new Plus grants in the next round

OpenAI 擴大「學術研究人員 ChatGPT」計劃，目標讓最多 20 萬名研究人員免費使用，下輪將再發 5 萬個 Plus 名額

OpenAI said on November 16 that it was expanding its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program to give up to 200,000 researchers worldwide free access to ChatGPT, double the goal it announced when the July pilot began. It said 20,000 researchers in 33 countries have joined since then. Applications for the next round open Monday, November 23, and 50,000 new grants will be awarded first-come, first-served to eligible researchers, replacing lottery-based selection. Each grant gives the recipient and up to two collaborators free access to ChatGPT Plus, covering as many as 150,000 people. Those already on the waitlist who qualify will be emailed before applications open with instructions for activating an account. Participants can use OpenAI’s frontier models and receive training on using ChatGPT in their research; by default, data shared through the program is not used to train models. The pilot offered ChatGPT Pro, but OpenAI said most participants would have stayed within Plus usage limits, so new grants will be Plus in order to reach more teams. The company’s Economic Research team also published Science at the Frontier: How Scientists Use ChatGPT, examining how more than 1,800 researchers used ChatGPT during the summer pilot; surveyed participants estimated that, without AI, the same research work at the same quality would have taken nearly twice as long.

OpenAI 於 11 月 16 日宣布，擴大 ChatGPT for Academic Researchers 計劃，目標讓全球最多 20 萬名研究人員免費使用 ChatGPT，較 7 月試點啟動時公布的目標增加一倍。公司稱，自 7 月試點以來，已有 33 個國家共 2 萬名研究人員加入。下一輪申請訂於 11 月 23 日（星期一）開放，將以先到先得方式向符合資格的研究人員發放 5 萬個新名額，不再以抽籤分配；每個名額涵蓋申請人及最多兩名合作者的免費 ChatGPT Plus，最多可覆蓋 15 萬人。若已在輪候名單且符合資格，公司會在申請開放前以電郵說明如何啟用帳戶。參與者可使用該公司前沿模型，並獲研究用途培訓；計劃預設不會把經此計劃分享的數據用於訓練模型。試點期間提供的是 ChatGPT Pro，但公司檢視用量後認為多數參與者會落在 Plus 限額內，故新一輪改為 Plus，以便覆蓋更多團隊。OpenAI 經濟研究團隊亦同時發布《Science at the Frontier: How Scientists Use ChatGPT》，整理逾 1,800 名研究人員在夏季試點中的使用情況；受訪者估計，若沒有人工智能而要達到相同質素，相關研究工作所需時間幾乎要多一倍。

5. Google updates the Gemini app so users can keep chat history while opting out of generative-AI training

Google 更新 Gemini 應用程式：可保留對話紀錄但選擇不把內容用於訓練生成式模型

Google said on November 16 that it was updating controls in the Gemini app so users can keep their Gemini chat history while opting out of having that history used to train generative AI models. Users will still be able to change how long history is saved and to review or delete history manually at any time. As a safety measure, if the new History setting is turned off, future chats will still be saved for around two months and used to respond to the user, process feedback, and protect Google, its users and the public. The changes will start rolling out globally in the Gemini app in the coming weeks. This is an update to privacy and safety controls, not a new model release.

Google 於 11 月 16 日宣布，將更新 Gemini 應用程式的對話紀錄及訓練控制，讓用戶保留 Gemini 對話紀錄，同時選擇不把這些紀錄用於訓練生成式人工智能模型。用戶仍可更改紀錄保存多久，並隨時檢視或手動刪除紀錄。為加強安全，若用戶關閉新的「History」設定，日後對話仍會保存約兩個月，用於回覆用戶、處理意見反饋，以及保護 Google、其用戶及公眾。有關改動將於未來數週在全球 Gemini 應用程式陸續推出。此為私隱與安全控制更新，並非新模型發布。

3. The U.S. Commerce Department issues an interim final rule limiting remote use of advanced AI chips at overseas data centers by companies in countries of concern

美國商務部公布臨時最終規則，限制受關注國家企業經海外數據中心遙距使用先進人工智能芯片

CNBC reported that the Commerce Department’s Bureau of Industry and Security moved Monday, through an interim final rule, to close a loophole that had allowed Chinese companies to lease advanced computing power from U.S. firms such as Nvidia at data centers in third countries, circumventing controls on exporting sensitive chips to China. Citing a BIS press release, CNBC said the rule was “written by and at the direction of” the Treasury Department and the White House National Security Council, and described it as the first U.S. policy response after Jensen Huang’s resignation last week. The rule says the new restrictions “will undermine the ability of companies headquartered in countries of concern from using” advanced AI semiconductors produced by U.S. companies to train advanced models. 5. DBS and J.P. Morgan’s Kinexys launch 24-hour transfers of tokenized dollar deposits, with Ant International as the first client

DBS 與摩根大通 Kinexys 推出全日 24 小時代幣化美元存款跨境轉賬，螞蟻國際成首名客戶

DBS said on November 16 that it and Kinexys by J.P. Morgan had launched what they called an industry-first, commercially ready interoperability solution connecting DBS Token Services with Kinexys’s account-based permissioned tokenized-deposit network. Institutional clients can now make instant transfers of tokenized U.S. dollar deposits between blockchain-based accounts in the United States and Singapore around the clock, including weekends and outside traditional banking hours. The solution uses Partior for settlement orchestration and advances Project BLOOM, an initiative led by the Monetary Authority of Singapore to enable the safe use of tokenized money; the two banks co-lead that project’s token-interoperability workgroup. Ant International is the first client, using the service for cross-border treasury and liquidity management. A footnote in the announcement said transfers on the Kinexys Blockchain Deposit Account network complete on a 24/7/365, same-day basis, but moving funds to and from traditional demand-deposit accounts still has a three-hour weekend downtime from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. ET every Saturday, with an enhancement under development. This is an institutional, permissioned transfer of deposit tokens, not a public cryptocurrency issuance.

新加坡星展銀行 11 月 16 日宣布，已與摩根大通的 Kinexys 推出據稱業界首個可商用的互通方案，連接星展 Token Services 與 Kinexys 的賬戶型許可代幣化存款網絡，讓機構客戶可在美國與新加坡的區塊鏈賬戶之間，全日 24 小時（包括週末及傳統銀行營業時間以外）即時劃撥代幣化美元存款。方案借助 Partior 進行結算協調，並推進新加坡金融管理局牽頭、旨在安全擴大代幣化貨幣應用的 Project BLOOM；兩家銀行共同領導該項目的代幣互通工作小組。螞蟻國際成為首名客戶，用於跨境財資與流動性管理。官方附註稱，Kinexys 區塊鏈存款賬戶網絡上的轉賬按 24/7/365、當日完成；但從傳統活期存款賬戶轉入或轉出該網絡，週六美東時間下午 3 時至 6 時仍有三小時停機，相關改進仍在開發。此為機構許可鏈上的存款代幣轉賬，並非公開交易的加密貨幣發行。
