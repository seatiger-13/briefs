1. OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki publishes “Perplexity and Intelligence,” proposing perplexity as a shared metric across labs, disclosing OpenAI’s internal pretraining progress, and calling for common development limits until monitoring reaches a defined milestone

OpenAI 首席科學家 Jakub Pachocki 發表《Perplexity and Intelligence》，建議以困惑度作為跨開發機構的共同指標，並公開該公司內部預訓練進度，同時倡議在監察能力達致明確里程碑前設定共同上限

OpenAI published an essay on 2026-11-22 by chief scientist Jakub Pachocki, “Perplexity and Intelligence.” He argued for establishing perplexity — or equivalently, cross-entropy loss — as a metric to compare model developers and inform shared safety standards, and, as a first step, disclosed OpenAI’s internal pretraining progress. Publishing those internal perplexity measurements “took some consideration,” he wrote, because it gives competitors a clearer picture of where the company stands, but that concern was outweighed by the urgency of establishing shared safety measures across the industry. The figures are reported on a held-out sample from OpenAI’s internal codebase, which the company has tracked since GPT-3.5: GPT-3.5, at a log-perplexity of about 0.99, was capable of fluent conversation and formed the basis for the original ChatGPT; GPT-4, at about 0.91, was proficient at using chain-of-thought reasoning; and once losses fell below 0.8, the company saw what he called transformative capabilities in fields such as cybersecurity and mathematics. After an upgrade to a model of log-perplexity around 0.76, he wrote, the model started producing groundbreaking results on open mathematics problems, with solutions to questions colleagues had long thought about appearing one after another in a single Slack thread. He said a similar sudden acceleration in pretraining algorithmic research could conceivably appear as early as 2027. Extrapolating from recent progress, he said further transformative capabilities should be expected as models enter a log-perplexity window of about 0.68 to 0.7, including capabilities that may not be tested for today, alongside further degradation in currently dominant forms of alignment auditing and greater evaluation and training awareness. He called on frontier developers to establish shared norms for evaluating such models and red-teaming safety cases by the time they are first used, and proposed common limits, based on perplexity and other measurements, that developers would not cross until the ability to understand and monitor AI behavior reached a concrete milestone, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. He also set out the difficulties: different labs use different evaluation datasets and training mixtures, so comparisons are hard; perplexity does not directly correspond to a task people care about; advances outside pretraining, notably chain-of-thought reasoning, do not reduce the metric; and the inherent entropy of the data — an unknown floor — is not known. The essay does not announce a specific numerical limit already agreed among labs, and this brief does not treat it as an industry agreement already in force.

OpenAI 於 2026-11-22 刊登首席科學家 Jakub Pachocki 的文章《Perplexity and Intelligence》。他主張把困惑度（perplexity，或等價的交叉熵損失）訂為比較各開發機構、並據以制定共同安全標準的指標；作為第一步，他公開了 OpenAI 內部的預訓練進度。他寫道，公布這些內部困惑度數字需要斟酌，因為競爭對手會更清楚該公司所處位置，但建立跨行業共同安全措施的迫切性更重要。文章以該公司自 GPT-3.5 起沿用的內部程式碼庫保留集為準：GPT-3.5 的對數困惑度約為 0.99，已能流暢對話並成為原版 ChatGPT 的基礎；GPT-4 約為 0.91，已能有效運用思維鏈推理；當損失低於 0.8 後，他們在網絡安全及數學等領域見到「變革性」能力。他稱，升級至對數困惑度約 0.76 的模型後，模型開始就開放數學問題給出突破性結果，有同事長年思考的問題在同一 Slack 討論串中相繼得到解答；他認為到 2027 年，預訓練演算法研究也可能出現類似的突然加速。按近期進度外推，他預期進入約 0.68 至 0.7 的對數困惑度區間時，還會出現目前未必已在測試的新能力，同時現時主流的對齊審計方式可能進一步失效，模型對評估及訓練的察覺亦會增加。他建議前沿開發機構在這類模型首次被使用前，共同訂立評估及紅隊測試安全論證的規範，並提出以困惑度及其他度量為基礎的共同上限：在理解及監察人工智能行為的能力達致具體里程碑（例如發展出思維鏈監察器的後繼方案）之前，不應跨越該上限。他同時承認挑戰：各實驗室使用不同評估數據集及訓練數據，難以直接比較；困惑度並非直接對應某項具體任務；思維鏈等預訓練以外的進展不會降低該指標；而且數據固有熵未知，指標存在未知下限。文章並未公布一個已獲各實驗室同意的具體上限數值，本簡報亦不把此文視為一項已達成的行業協議。
