5.

DeepMind 以 AlphaProof Code 形式化驗證 Signal 協議實作

Google DeepMind 發表 AlphaProof Code，稱在人類專家指導下，以 Gemini Carbon 等代理用四週寫出逾 600 萬行 Lean 證明，形式化驗證 Signal 協議實際程式碼的關鍵安全性質（包括裝置短暫被入侵前後的訊息保護），並稱這是首次以任何方法、在此深度驗證廣泛部署協議的實作；系統亦能證明被重新植入的已知漏洞版本不安全。Signal 技術總監 Ehren Kret 稱有助加強對私隱保證的信心；相關形式化產物將開源。

DeepMind’s AlphaProof Code formally verifies Signal’s protocol implementation

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, saying human-guided agents using Gemini Carbon produced more than six million lines of Lean proofs in four weeks and formally verified key security properties of Signal’s actual cryptographic code—including protections before and after a temporary device compromise. DeepMind called it the first time a widely deployed protocol’s implementation has been verified at this depth, by any method; the system also proved that two reintroduced, previously fixed bugs made an isolated copy insecure. Signal CTO Ehren Kret said it increases confidence in the protocol’s privacy guarantees; artifacts are being open-sourced.

來源／Source: Google DeepMind — https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/

NVIDIA releases Alpamayo 2 Edge for onboard autonomous driving

NVIDIA introduced Alpamayo 2 Edge, a 2-billion-parameter adaptive-reasoning vision-language-action model built for real-time use on DRIVE AGX Thor. 熱 CPI 打擊加密市場；期貨爆倉約 $7.16 億

十月通脹高於預期後，風險資產同步受壓：比特幣跌穿約 $74,000，並一度接近 $73,000；FXStreet 引述 Coinglass，過去 24 小時加密期貨爆倉約 $7.16 億，其中多頭約 $5.89 億。Investing.com 引述周二美東約 17:29，比特幣跌約 2.2% 報 $73,649，以太坊跌約 4%，XRP 與 Solana 亦明顯下跌。交易商 Wintermute 指約 $73,000（接近 200 日均線）若不能守住，下一個焦點或看約 $68,000。

Hot CPI hits crypto; futures liquidations about $716 million

After the hotter-than-expected CPI report, bitcoin broke below about $74,000 and traded near $73,000. FXStreet, citing Coinglass, said about $716 million in crypto futures were liquidated over 24 hours, including about $589 million in long positions. Investing.com put bitcoin around $73,649 as of about 5:29 p.m. ET Tuesday, down about 2.2%, with ether off about 4% and XRP and Solana also lower. Trading firm Wintermute flagged about $73,000—near the 200-day average—as important support, warning that a daily close below it could open a move toward about $68,000.

2.

美國現貨比特幣 ETF 周一再錄約 $2.01 億淨流出

CoinDesk 引述 Farside Investors，美國現貨比特幣 ETF 周一淨流出約 $2.01 億，當中貝萊德 IBIT 流出約 $2.132 億，已連續第七個交易日錄得贖回；同日現貨以太坊 ETF 淨流出約 $1.846 億。資金持續撤走，削弱機構買盤對價格的支撐。

U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs saw about $201 million of net outflows Monday

CoinDesk, citing Farside Investors, said U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $201 million on Monday, led by about $213.2 million of outflows from BlackRock’s IBIT, its seventh straight session of redemptions. U.S. spot ether ETFs lost about $184.6 million the same day. Persistent outflows have left less institutional bid under the market.

來源／Source: CoinDesk — https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/10/bitcoin-lags-stock-rebound-in-advance-of-u-s-inflation-report

3.

以太坊現貨 ETF 連六週流出，累計逾約 $18 億

The Block 在 11 月 10 日的數據評論指出，美國現貨以太坊 ETF 已連續六週淨流出，累計超過約 $18 億，約相當於資產規模的 15%；今年以來以太坊 ETF 流出金額約為比特幣 ETF 的 2.8 倍，儘管其規模只約後者的八分之一。上週比特幣與以太坊現貨 ETF 合計流出亦超過 $10 億，比特幣端主要集中在 IBIT，富達與 Bitwise 則錄得流入。

Ethereum spot ETFs post a sixth week of outflows, totaling more than ~$1.8 billion

The Block’s Nov. 10 data note said U.S. spot ether ETFs have had six straight weeks of outflows totaling more than about $1.8 billion, or roughly 15% of assets. Year-to-date, ether ETFs have shed about 2.8 times as much as bitcoin ETFs despite being only about an eighth the size. Combined spot bitcoin and ether ETF outflows exceeded $1 billion last week, with bitcoin outflows concentrated in IBIT while Fidelity and Bitwise saw inflows.

來源／Source: The Block — https://www.theblock.co/newsletters/data-and-insights/2026-11-10-crypto-etf-carnage-stablecoin-contraction-437268

4.

穩定幣供應出現近年少見的收縮

同一篇 The Block 評論指，USDT 與 USDC 供應均在下降：今年迄今 USDC 約減 6.67%、USDT 約減 5.4%；過去一週穩定幣總供應約跌 1.4%，為 2023 年初以來最大降幅。評論認為這反映大額資金正離開鏈上、轉回傳統渠道，而以太坊上的穩定幣餘額現約 $670 億，今年已跌逾 20%，並已被 Tron 超前。

Stablecoin supply posts a rare contraction

The same The Block note said both USDT and USDC have been shrinking: USDC is down about 6.67% year-to-date and USDT about 5.4%, while total stablecoin supply fell about 1.4% last week, the largest decline since early 2023. The authors read this as large holders leaving crypto for traditional rails. Stablecoin balances on Ethereum were put at about $67 billion, down more than 20% this year, with Tron now ahead of Ethereum.

來源／Source: The Block — https://www.theblock.co/newsletters/data-and-insights/2026-11-10-crypto-etf-carnage-stablecoin-contraction-437268

5.

倫敦交易所修訂加密貨幣 ETN 上市資料，即日生效

倫敦證券交易所 11 月 10 日發出通知（N12/26），修訂加密貨幣交易所買賣票據（cETN）資料頁：對僅限專業投資者分部的部分上市準則有所放寬，但仍規定相關資產僅限比特幣及／或以太坊，且託管人須在可接受司法管轄區受反清洗黑錢監管。修訂自當日起生效。

London Stock Exchange eases some crypto ETN rules, effective Nov. 10

The London Stock Exchange issued notice N12/26 on Nov. 10 amending its crypto exchange-traded notes factsheet. Some admission criteria were removed for securities on professional-investor-only segments, while the underlying assets remain limited to bitcoin and/or ether and custodians must still be subject to AML regulation in permitted jurisdictions. The changes took effect the same day.
