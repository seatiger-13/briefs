Anthropic secures about $3.4 billion generator deal for AI data centers

The Financial Times reported that Anthropic bought commitments for 1.7 GW of heavy-duty gas-fired generators from OnePWR Solutions, including installation capabilities. It shows AI labs are securing their own power to meet demand, a sign AI infrastructure spending keeps growing. (Reuters could not independently verify the report.)

3. Binance 確認年底前關閉部分歐盟客戶帳戶

因未能取得歐盟 MiCA 牌照、過渡期屆滿，Binance 確認於 12月31日前關閉受影響歐洲帳戶，法國、西班牙、意大利及波蘭用戶已收到通知；存款已停，可提款，11月29日起餘額每月收 0.025% 或 10 USDC 費用。反映歐盟監管對大型交易所嘅實際影響。

Binance confirms closures of some EU customer accounts by year-end

Having not obtained EU MiCA authorization as the transition period expired, Binance confirmed it will close affected European accounts by Dec 31; users in France, Spain, Italy and Poland got notices, deposits are blocked but withdrawals remain open, and from Nov 29 balances incur a monthly 0.025% or 10 USDC fee. It shows MiCA's real bite on major exchanges.

Vitalik says ETH should be valued as a store of value, not a fee-burning token

Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin argued on Sunday that ETH's value should rest on decentralization, censorship resistance and security as a store of value; he backs keeping EIP-1559's burn but said fee burning cannot set a reliable valuation floor. It marks a notable shift in Ethereum's value narrative.
