2. OpenAI published a November 20 article describing how it detects and disrupts unauthorized distillation intended to reproduce frontier-model capabilities.

OpenAI 公布加強防範「敵對蒸餾」（adversarial distillation）的措施，

It said an adversary can use a frontier model’s outputs to train another model with similar capabilities but without comparable safeguards,

稱在內部測試中，

a risk it said is especially acute in dual-use domains such as cybersecurity and biology.

從 GPT-6.2 Astra 提取受保護推理過程的攻擊成功率由 54.0% 降至 0.2%

The company placed particular emphasis on preventing extraction of protected reasoning — the internal chain-of-thought — because those intermediate traces can contain information absent from the final answer and are especially valuable training data for another model.

OpenAI said it had strengthened these defenses in GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol.

In an internal adaptive evaluation using strong automated red-teaming,

it tested whether attackers could recover protected reasoning the model had generated while answering an earlier prompt;

the average attack success rate fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra with its original safeguards to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra with the updated safeguards.

The same day, an update to OpenAI’s Usage Policies took effect,

including a clarification of existing restrictions on using its models and outputs to train models intended as alternatives to or substitutes for OpenAI models or services.

OpenAI 於 11 月 20 日發表文章，說明如何偵測及打斷未經授權、用以複製前沿模型能力的蒸餾行為。

公司指，攻擊者可利用前沿模型的輸出訓練另一模型，使其具備相近能力卻沒有相應安全防護，在網絡安全及生物學等兩用領域尤其令人擔憂；公司特別強調要防止攻擊者提取模型的受保護推理過程（即內部思維鏈），因為這些中間步驟可能包含最終答案未顯示的資訊，對訓練另一模型尤其有價值。

OpenAI 稱已在 GPT-6.2 Astra 及 GPT-6.2 Sol 加強防護。

在一項使用強力自動化紅隊的內部適應性評估中，公司測試攻擊者能否取回模型在較早提示中生成的受保護推理；平均攻擊成功率由原先防護下 GPT-6 Astra 的 54.0%，降至更新防護後 GPT-6.2 Astra 的 0.2%。

同一日，OpenAI 使用政策更新生效，當中澄清不得利用其模型及輸出，訓練作為 OpenAI 模型或服務替代品的模型。
