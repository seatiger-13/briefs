citing up to 14% PPA improvement on AMD’s flagship GPU IP,

稱與 AMD 合作使其旗艦 GPU 知識產權的功耗、效能及面積改善最多 14%，

with some changes already sent for manufacturing

In a November 18 post, OpenAI described how its models are improving the chips that power AI, and how it is applying the same approach to third-party hardware.

OpenAI 於 11 月 18 日刊出專文，說明其模型如何改進人工智能芯片，以及如何把同一方法應用於第三方硬件。

Working with AMD, it said it had seen up to 14% improvement in power, performance and area against heavily optimized baselines on AMD’s flagship GPU IP.

公司稱，與 AMD 合作後，相對已高度優化的基準，其旗艦 GPU 知識產權的功耗、效能及面積（PPA）改善最多 14%；模型透過改寫描述電路的 SystemVerilog RTL 代碼找出進一步改進，並稱這相當於「在不足 5 個月內取得約一年的進展」。

The model identified further improvements by rewriting the SystemVerilog RTL that describes the circuits, results OpenAI said represented “roughly a year of progress achieved in less than 5 months.”

OpenAI 表示，AMD 已把部分改動納入近期送交製造的設計，並把其他改動加入未來版本的生產設計檔。

AMD has already included some of the changes in a recent design sent for manufacturing, and added others to production design files for future versions, OpenAI said.

AMD 亦核實，在其下一代旗艦 CPU 的關鍵區塊上，由該模型撰寫的設計帶來單位數百分比的 PPA 增益。

AMD also verified single-digit percentage PPA gains across key blocks of its upcoming next-generation flagship CPU, using designs written by the model.

在 OpenAI 自行的 Jalapeño 推理芯片上，團隊估計模型建議的改動使標準單元面積在向量單元減少約 13%、在矩陣單元減少約 10%，並稱這些優化已在硅驗證中得到證實。

On OpenAI’s own Jalapeño inference chip, the team estimated that model-proposed changes reduced standard-cell area by about 13% in the vector unit and 10% in the matrix unit, optimizations it said were silicon-proven.

公司並稱正與 Synopsys 合作訓練 GPT-Synopsys，讓模型按專家方式使用電子設計自動化工具。

OpenAI also said it is partnering with Synopsys to train GPT-Synopsys to use electronic design automation tools the way expert chip designers do.

並披露已出售全部企業財庫以太幣及一半 Solana，

套現 1,930 萬美元，

為最多 4,000 萬美元的股份回購提供資金

CoinShares on November 18 reported unaudited results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, with net income of $39.6 million, up from $31.2 million a year earlier, principally because of a $14.4 million gain on treasury digital assets.

CoinShares 於 11 月 18 日公布截至 2026 年 9 月 30 日的第三季未經審核業績，淨利潤 3,960 萬美元，高於上年同期的 3,120 萬美元，主要因為企業財庫數碼資產錄得 1,440 萬美元收益。

Assets under management were $7.4 billion at period-end, even with the end of 2025 and up from $5.5 billion at June 30.

期末管理資產 74 億美元，與 2025 年底持平，但高於 6 月底的 55 億美元；季度收入 2,770 萬美元，低於上年同期的 5,030 萬美元。

Quarterly revenue was $27.7 million, down from $50.3 million a year earlier.

公司在業績中披露，已出售全部企業財庫以太坊持倉（1,660 萬美元）及一半企業財庫 Solana 持倉（270 萬美元），合共騰出 1,930 萬美元，接近 10 月 22 日董事會批准、最多 4,000 萬美元股份回購計劃的一半。

In the same release, the company said it had sold its entire corporate treasury position in ether, for $16.6 million, and half of its corporate treasury position in Solana, for $2.7 million, freeing up $19.3 million, nearly half of the $40 million share-repurchase program the board approved on October 22.

4. CoinDesk, citing CryptoQuant,

CoinDesk 引述 CryptoQuant：短期持倉者浮虧程度為 7 月以來最深，

says short-term holders are the deepest underwater since July,

比特幣較這批持倉者平均買入價低約 20%

with bitcoin about 20% below that cohort’s average purchase price

CoinDesk reported Wednesday, citing a weekly report CryptoQuant shared with the publication, that bitcoin’s drop to a four-month low of about $61,000 on November 15 has left short-term traders holding coins for one to three months with their biggest unrealized losses since July.

CoinDesk 於週三引述 CryptoQuant 向該媒體分享的週報稱，比特幣 11 月 15 日跌至四個月低位約 61,000 美元後，持倉一至三個月的短期交易者浮虧程度為 7 月以來最深，市場處於「價值區」，但這並不代表已經見底。

The firm said that puts the market in a “value zone” without marking a bottom.

該機構追蹤這批最能反映活躍交易者的持倉者平均買入價，並指比特幣當時較該水平低約 20%，為 7 月 1 日以來最大差距。

It tracks the average purchase price of that one-to-three-month cohort, which it says best reflects active traders, and said bitcoin was then trading about 20% below that level, the widest gap since July 1.

CryptoQuant 稱，低於負 12% 的讀數，歷史上往往與牛市回調的底部相近，但這些底部通常至少要兩個月才形成。

Readings below negative 12% have historically lined up with bottoms in bull-market pullbacks, CryptoQuant said, though those bottoms have usually taken at least two months to form.

5. CoinDesk: crypto’s basis trade has all but dried up,

CoinDesk：加密市場的基差交易幾乎消失，

and some traders are chasing stock perpetuals instead

部分交易員轉而追逐股票永續合約

CoinDesk reported on Wednesday that one of crypto’s steadier sources of return, the basis trade, has all but disappeared.

CoinDesk 週三報道，加密市場一項較穩定的套利來源——基差交易——已幾乎消失。

In that trade, investors buy bitcoin and short perpetual futures, earning a recurring fee from traders who are betting on higher prices, whether prices rise or fall, as long as those buyers keep paying the funding rate.

在這種交易中，投資者買入比特幣並同時做空永續期貨，只要渴望做多的交易者繼續支付資金費率，無論價格升跌都可賺取費用。

With those returns drying up, the report said, some traders have turned to stock perpetual contracts instead.

報道稱，隨着這類回報乾涸，部分交易員轉而追逐股票永續合約。

This is Wednesday’s market-structure observation, not a single-token price event; the latest snapshot is in the price section.
