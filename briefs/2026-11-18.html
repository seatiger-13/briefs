OpenAI 推出 GPT-6.2 Astra 與 Sol，指在專業工作上質素提升、成本下降，並即日起在 ChatGPT Work、Codex 及 API 提供

OpenAI 於 11 月 17 日宣布推出 GPT-6.2 Astra 與 GPT-6.2 Sol。公司稱 Astra 為應付最艱難項目的新前沿模型，在簡報、試算表、文件及編碼（尤其網站、遊戲及 3D 設計）上更貼近專業慣例；Sol 則為日常工作的高效率通用模型，並指兩者都能以較少 output token 完成要求較高的任務。官方稱，在測試多步專業工作流程的 Agents’ Last Exam 上，GPT-6.2 Astra 較 GPT-6 Astra 高 5.2 分，成本不到三分之一，亦較 Claude Fable 5.2 高 5.5 分，成本僅約 5%。公司並稱 Astra 在 Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 及 Vals Science Index 上刷新高位。API 方面，Astra 的 input 與 output 定價較 GPT-6 Astra 低 20%，cached input 再享 60% 折扣，標準費率為每百萬 token input 8 美元、cached input 0.40 美元、output 40 美元；Sol 維持 GPT-6.1 Sol 的每百萬 token input 2 美元、cached input 0.10 美元、output 10 美元。兩款模型即日起向所有 Plus、Pro、Enterprise 及 Edu 用戶在 ChatGPT Work 與 Codex 開放，開發者亦可經 API 使用 gpt-6.2-astra 及 gpt-6.2-sol。

Introducing GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol: OpenAI says professional quality is higher and costs are lower, with availability in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API from today

OpenAI on November 17 introduced GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. It described Astra as its new frontier model for the hardest projects, saying its slides, spreadsheets, documents and code — especially for websites, games and 3D design — more closely follow professional conventions, and Sol as an efficient, versatile model for everyday work. Both models, it said, deliver better results on demanding tasks with fewer output tokens. On Agents’ Last Exam, which tests multistep professional work, OpenAI said GPT-6.2 Astra scored 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra at less than a third of the cost, and 5.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at about 5% of the cost. It also said Astra set new highs on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 and the Vals Science Index. In the API, Astra’s input and output prices are 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra’s, with a 60% discount on cached input, at $8, $0.40 and $40 per million tokens respectively; Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol’s rates of $2, $0.10 and $10. Both models are available from today to all Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and to developers via the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol.

Microsoft 在 Ignite 推出 MAI Code 2、Code 2 Flash 及 Cyber 2 Flash，稱以較低成本應付企業編碼與保安工作

Microsoft 於 11 月 17 日在三藩市 Ignite 大會宣布新一批自有 MAI 模型。官方稱，旗艦代理編碼模型 MAI Code 2 面向把程式碼投入生產、深度除錯、多檔修改、倉庫級推理及介面工作；Code 2 Flash 則用於程式碼解釋、針對性修改、例行修復及快速迭代。公司表示，在四項常用企業編碼基準的平均值上，MAI Code 2 達到 GPT-6.1 Sol 約 98% 的表現、成本約為 40%，以及 Claude Opus 5.5 約 96% 的表現、成本約為 5%；上述成本比較部分採用其 50% 促銷價，官方亦註明模型尚未針對基礎科學研究或極長週期代理工作調校。Code 2 已在 GitHub Copilot 全面提供，Code 2 Flash 亦已在 Copilot 上線，並在 Microsoft Foundry 作有限預覽。同日推出的 Cyber 2 Flash 被稱為其迄今最有能力的保安模型，定位為以較低每項任務成本、在漏洞發現與修復上達到或超過部分前沿模型的表現，並與 Perception 等保安產品整合。

Microsoft introduces MAI Code 2, Code 2 Flash and Cyber 2 Flash at Ignite, pitching lower-cost models for enterprise coding and security

Microsoft on November 17, at its Ignite conference in San Francisco, announced new models in its MAI family. It said flagship agentic coding model MAI Code 2 is aimed at taking code to production, deep debugging, multi-file changes, repo-wide reasoning and user-interface work, while Code 2 Flash is designed for explanations, targeted edits, routine fixes and rapid iteration. Averaged over four widely used enterprise coding benchmarks, Microsoft said MAI Code 2 delivered 98% of the performance of GPT-6.1 Sol at 40% of the cost, and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5’s performance at 5% of the cost; some of those cost comparisons use a 50% promotional price, and the company noted the models are not yet tuned for fundamental scientific research or very long-horizon agentic work. Code 2 is generally available in GitHub Copilot, and Code 2 Flash is in Copilot and in limited-access preview in Microsoft Foundry. The company also introduced Cyber 2 Flash, which it called its most capable security model yet, saying the compact model can outperform frontier models on vulnerability discovery and remediation at a fraction of the cost per task, and is being integrated with products including Perception.

Microsoft 宣布 Perception 全面上市，並推出 Decision-1.1、Copilot Workspaces 及人工智能透明度計劃

在同一場 Ignite 主題演講中，Microsoft 主席兼行政總裁 Satya Nadella 宣布，代理保安系統 Perception 現已全面上市。該系統以紅隊、藍隊及綠隊代理編組，分別模擬攻擊、偵測攻擊以及修復，並可按 Playbook 在人工監督下自動觸發行動。公司並稱，Microsoft-Decision-1.1 已全面提供，新增多模態支援，並指已有逾 4,000 名客戶使用；GPT-6.2 及 Meta 最新模型亦將登陸 Foundry。Nadella 又推出 Copilot Workspaces，作為讓人員與其代理及其他同事在共享背景下協作的「多人」介面，以及把組織智能外化為平台的 Microsoft IQ，並宣布 Agent 365 Business Analytics，用以把人工智能開支與成果對照。公司同日公布 Microsoft AI Transparency Initiative，表示將擴大與美國國家標準與技術研究院（NIST）代理標準計劃的合作，並稱已有 40 個組織參與訂立相關標準；另宣布下一代 Cobalt 200 虛擬機全面上市，以及 Maia 300 晶片據稱較領先 GPU 系統每瓦特可輸出多 40% 的 token。

Microsoft makes Perception generally available and announces Decision-1.1, Copilot Workspaces and an AI transparency initiative

In the same Ignite keynote, Microsoft Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella said Perception, the company’s agentic security system, is now generally available. It uses teams of red, blue and green agents to simulate attacks, detect them and remediate, with playbooks that can trigger action autonomously under human control. Microsoft-Decision-1.1 is now generally available with multimodal support, and the company said more than 4,000 customers have already used it; GPT-6.2 and Meta’s latest models are also coming to Foundry. Nadella introduced Copilot Workspaces as a “multiplayer” workspace in which people, their agents and colleagues share context, and Microsoft IQ as a platform that externalizes an organization’s intelligence. He also announced Agent 365 Business Analytics, intended to connect AI spending to outcomes, and the Microsoft AI Transparency Initiative, under which Microsoft said it would expand its work in NIST’s Agent Standards Initiative and that 40 organizations were already involved in defining standards. The company also said next-generation Cobalt 200 virtual machines are generally available, and that its Maia 300 chip would deliver 40% more tokens per watt than leading GPU-based systems.

Google 宣布 Gemini「Tasks」即日起在美國、日本、韓國、加拿大、澳洲及印度免費推出，由 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 驅動

Google 於 11 月 17 日宣布，用戶可把更多任務交給 Gemini 處理，有關更新即日起在美國、日本、韓國、加拿大、澳洲及印度向數以億計用戶免費推出，並使用新的 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 模型；一套進階功能則先在美國推出。官方舉例指，Gemini 可代為致電商戶預約、分析電費單、比較商品並以 Google Pay 結帳，以及經 RCS 把購物清單加到 Instacart。公司稱，執行任務時會在 Google Cloud 的 Agent Compute Engine 內、於專屬於該帳戶、硬體隔離的虛擬機中運行，並設計為在「購買」等關鍵步驟前先徵求確認；電話功能目前在印第安納、路易斯安那、明尼蘇達、蒙大拿及內布拉斯加州不可用。The Verge 及 TechCrunch 報道指，這項能力此前稱為 Spark，現已更名為 Tasks。用戶可在 Gemini 主畫面把「Chat」切換至「Task」。此為助手功能擴大及免費供應範圍的更新，並非新基礎模型發布。

Google makes Gemini Tasks free in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India, powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen

Google said on November 17 that people can now hand off more work to Gemini, with the update rolling out free of charge to hundreds of millions of people in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India, using its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. A set of advanced features is rolling out first in the U.S. Official examples included calling dealerships to book a service appointment, analyzing a utility bill, comparing products and checking out with Google Pay, and adding groceries to an Instacart order by RCS. Google said tasks run in hardware-isolated virtual machines dedicated to the user’s account on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine, and that Gemini is designed to pause for confirmation before critical steps such as clicking “Buy.” Calling through Gemini is not currently available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana and Nebraska. The Verge and TechCrunch reported that the capability, previously known as Spark, has been renamed Tasks. Users can switch the Gemini home-screen toggle from Chat to Task. This is an expansion of assistant features and free access, not a new foundation-model release.

OpenAI 公布與北約及盟國網絡機構的合作，透過 Daybreak 向可信防禦方提供網絡安全模型

OpenAI 於 11 月 17 日刊出專文，說明正透過 Daybreak，把先進人工智能交到獲授權的防禦人員手上，用於審查軟件、調查可疑活動、核實漏洞以及開發和測試修補。公司稱與北約通訊及資訊局（NCIA）合作，向北約核心機構提供先進網絡安全模型，以協助保護盟國網絡，並指北約在有關工作中每月使用多達 3,000 億個原始 input 及 output token。NCIA 總經理 Dylan Browne 稱，有關模型可加快發現漏洞、以機器速度偵測新威脅，並有助優化成本與採購。OpenAI 並稱曾支援北約合作網絡防禦卓越中心舉辦的 2026 年 Crossed Swords 演習，當中新增以 Daybreak 訓練學員建構、部署及評估網絡行動人工智能代理、同時保持人為監督的課程。此外，公司表示已向歐盟網絡安全局（ENISA）以及法國、德國、波蘭等國家機構的防禦人員提供有關模型；並稱烏克蘭數碼轉型部正使用 Daybreak 加強民用基礎設施的網絡防禦。

OpenAI details work with NATO and allied cyber agencies, putting cybersecurity models in defenders’ hands through Daybreak

In a November 17 post, OpenAI said it is using Daybreak to put advanced AI in the hands of trusted defenders for authorized security work, including reviewing software, investigating suspicious activity, validating vulnerabilities, and developing and testing fixes. It said it works with NATO’s Communications and Information Agency to provide NATO’s core institutions with access to advanced cybersecurity models to help protect Allied networks, and that NATO has used up to 300 billion raw input and output tokens a month in that work. NCIA General Manager Dylan Browne said the models accelerate vulnerability discovery, detect emerging threats at machine speed and help the alliance understand costs and procurement. OpenAI also said it supported the 2026 Crossed Swords exercise organized by NATO’s Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence, which included a new training stream in which participants built, deployed and evaluated AI agents for cyber operations with Daybreak, while keeping human oversight. Beyond NATO, it said it had already provided access to defenders at the EU’s ENISA and national agencies in France, Germany and Poland, and that Ukraine’s Ministry of Digital Transformation is using Daybreak to strengthen cyber defense of civilian infrastructure.

CoinDesk 於 11 月 17 日報道，比特幣自週末低位反彈後進展有限，當時自 UTC 午夜起再跌 1.0%，約報 64,200 美元，並低於約 68,000 美元的 200 週均線。做市商 Wintermute 稱，價格有可能滑落至 5 萬美元區間，理由包括市場對 12 月聯儲加息的定價當時約為 92%、十年期美債孳息重上 5%，以及穩定幣供應收縮和數碼資產財政儲備公司需求減弱。報道並指，加密期貨成交在 24 小時內回升 29.7% 至 1,798 億美元，未平倉合約升 3.0% 至 1,110 億美元，清算額升 57.1% 至 2.796 億美元；Binance、OKX 及 Bybit 上多數幣種的資金費率仍為負，顯示倉位仍然偏空。最新價格見行情一節。週末低位及當時的大規模清算已在較早簡報述及，本則只記錄其後價格停滯及衍生品倉位的新變化。

Bitcoin remains penned in near $64,000, below its roughly $68,000 200-week average; Wintermute warns it could slide into the $50,000s

CoinDesk reported on November 17 that bitcoin had made little headway since its weekend rebound, and was down a further 1.0% since midnight UTC at around $64,200, well below its 200-week average of about $68,000. Market maker Wintermute said the price could slide into the $50,000s, citing a December Fed rate increase that markets then put at around 92%, a 10-year Treasury yield back above 5%, shrinking stablecoin supply and weaker demand from digital-asset treasury companies. CoinDesk also said crypto futures volume rose 29.7% over 24 hours to $179.8 billion, open interest increased 3.0% to $111.0 billion, and liquidations rose 57.1% to $279.6 million. Funding remained negative for most coins on Binance, OKX and Bybit, a sign of still-bearish positioning. The latest snapshot is in the price section. The weekend low and the earlier wave of liquidations were covered in prior briefs; this item records the subsequent stall and the new derivatives positioning.

美國現貨比特幣 ETF 週一淨流入 790 萬美元，結束連續七日流出；貝萊德 IBIT 流入 7,650 萬美元，結束連續 11 個流出交易日

CoinDesk 引述 Farside Investors 報道，美國現貨比特幣 ETF 在 11 月 16 日（星期一）錄得 790 萬美元淨流入，結束連續七日淨流出。貝萊德 IBIT 當日流入 7,650 萬美元，結束連續 11 個流出交易日；但其他基金的流出抵銷了大部分流入，其中灰度 GBTC 流出 3,460 萬美元。昨日簡報在截稿時尚未見到已核實的週一完整資金流數字；本則記錄這項其後公布的週一數據。流入規模仍然很小，且被其他基金的贖回大幅對沖，因此這次轉正並不代表需求已全面恢復。

U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs took in a net $7.9 million on Monday, ending a seven-day outflow streak; BlackRock’s IBIT added $76.5 million, ending an 11-session run of outflows

CoinDesk, citing Farside Investors, reported that U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs took in a net $7.9 million on Monday, November 16, ending seven straight sessions of net outflows. BlackRock’s IBIT drew $76.5 million, ending an 11-session outflow streak, though outflows from other funds offset most of that inflow, led by $34.6 million from Grayscale’s GBTC. Yesterday’s brief had no verified full figure for Monday’s flows by the cutoff; this item records that subsequently published Monday print. The net inflow was modest and was largely offset by redemptions from other funds, so the turn higher does not by itself show a broad recovery in demand.

Wells Fargo 披露截至 9 月底持有貝萊德 IBIT 4,958 萬股，價值 23.5 億美元，三個月內持股數量增加一倍以上

CoinDesk 報道，Wells Fargo 在週一提交的文件中披露，截至 9 月 30 日持有貝萊德 iShares Bitcoin Trust（IBIT）4,958 萬股，價值 23.5 億美元，較 6 月底的 2,440 萬股、當時約值 8.12 億美元，持股數量增加逾一倍。按貝萊德季度披露，IBIT 當時有 14.15 億股、持有 801,033 枚比特幣，有關持股相當於間接曝險約 28,070 枚比特幣。報道強調，文件涵蓋該行及其相關投資經理申報的證券，包括代客戶管理的倉位，並不能確定有多少屬於銀行自身資產負債表；這是六週後才披露的 9 月 30 日快照，亦無法顯示這些帳戶在近日比特幣由約 74,000 美元跌至約 63,000 美元、以及 IBIT 流出超過 10 億美元期間有否增持或減持。報道並指，在已彙編的 13F 數據中，Wells Fargo 是申報 IBIT 持倉最多的投資經理，排在 Millennium、Jane Street 及摩根士丹利之前。

Wells Fargo discloses 49.58 million shares of BlackRock’s IBIT, worth $2.35 billion, as of September 30, more than doubling its share count in three months

CoinDesk reported that Wells Fargo disclosed in a filing Monday that it held 49.58 million shares of BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) at the end of September, worth $2.35 billion, up from 24.4 million shares worth about $812 million at the end of June, more than doubling the share count in three months. Based on BlackRock’s quarterly disclosure that IBIT held 801,033 bitcoin against 1.415 billion shares outstanding on September 30, the position represented indirect exposure to about 28,070 bitcoin. CoinDesk stressed that the filing covers securities reported by the bank and its associated investment managers, including positions managed for clients, and does not establish how much belongs to the bank itself. It is a September 30 snapshot disclosed six weeks later and does not show whether those accounts added or cut holdings during bitcoin’s recent decline from about $74,000 to about $63,000, a period when IBIT outflows exceeded $1 billion. In compiled 13F data, Wells Fargo was the largest reported IBIT holder among investment managers, ahead of Millennium Management, Jane Street and Morgan Stanley, the report said.

與 Binance 遭竊資金相關的錢包在兩週沉寂後，再轉出約 3.43 萬枚以太幣，價值近 6,300 萬美元

CoinDesk 於 11 月 17 日報道，與 10 月 29 日 Binance 遭竊約 63 億美元相關的一個地址，在超過兩週沒有活動後，向三個新錢包轉出 34,279.66 枚以太幣，價值近 6,300 萬美元。報道引述區塊鏈保安公司 MistTrack 及 Solid Intel 週二在 X 的帖文，並指截至報道時，這些以太幣尚未再轉出；三個新錢包亦尚未被連繫到交易所或洗錢服務，單憑轉帳並不能確定攻擊者是否準備出售，還是只在其控制的地址之間調動。聯邦調查局將是次盜竊歸因於朝鮮的 TraderTraitor 組織。報道並引述 Elliptic 較早估計，約 28.5 億美元被盜資產已被凍結或扣押，攻擊者仍控制約 32.8 億美元，分散在逾 950 個錢包。上週司法部獲准扣押或限制逾 12 億美元相關資產，已在較早簡報述及，本則只記錄週二這批新的鏈上轉移。

A wallet tied to the Binance theft moves 34,280 ether, worth nearly $63 million, after two weeks of silence

CoinDesk reported on November 17 that an address linked to the October 29 theft of about $6.3 billion from Binance transferred 34,279.66 ether, worth nearly $63 million, to three newly created wallets after more than two weeks without activity. Citing Tuesday posts on X by blockchain security firms MistTrack and Solid Intel, it said the ether had not moved again at the time of the report, and that the new wallets had not been linked to an exchange or a laundering service; the transfers alone did not establish whether the attackers intended to sell the ether or were simply moving it between addresses they control. The FBI has attributed the theft to North Korea’s TraderTraitor group. CoinDesk also cited an earlier Elliptic estimate that about $2.85 billion of the stolen assets had been frozen or seized, leaving the attackers in control of roughly $3.28 billion across more than 950 wallets. Last week’s court approval for the Justice Department to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion was covered in an earlier brief; this item records Tuesday’s new on-chain transfers only.
