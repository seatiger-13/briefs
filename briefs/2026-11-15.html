SpaceX 週六首次從軌道接住 Starship 上層級；週五助推器改為海上濺落並在入水前爆炸

CNBC 週六報道，SpaceX 在得州 Starbase 用發射塔上的「筷子」機械臂，成功接住從軌道返回的 Starship 上層級（報道稱為 Starship 42），為該公司首次完成此項操作。這次第 15 次試飛原計劃同時接住助推器與上層級；週五第一階段未能接住助推器，SpaceX 改為將其導向墨西哥灣濺落，助推器在入水前爆炸。上層級其後繞地球 14 圈，再於綽號 Mechazilla 的發射塔成功降落。馬斯克其後在 X 發布檢查該上層級的影片。試飛週五亦投放另外 26 顆最新的 Starlink v3 衛星；CNBC 指星座在近地軌道已有逾 11,000 顆衛星，一枚 Starship 最多可運載 60 顆 v3 衛星。夏威夷運輸部門週五曾因碎片風險，暫時暫停部分西岸航班；FAA 另因電訊系統故障發出罕見的全國地面停飛令，兩項限制其後均已解除。本則未採用未經同一報道核實的盤後股價變動。

SpaceX catches Starship’s upper stage from orbit for the first time on Saturday, after Friday’s booster was diverted to a splashdown and exploded

CNBC reported on Saturday that SpaceX used the “chopsticks” mechanical arms on its launch tower at Starbase, Texas, to catch Starship’s returning upper stage, which the report identified as Starship 42, the first time the company has completed that recovery. Test Flight 15 was the first attempt to catch both the booster and the upper stage. In the first phase on Friday, SpaceX failed to catch the booster and diverted it to a Gulf of Mexico splashdown, where it exploded before hitting the water. The upper stage then flew 14 times around the Earth before landing on the launch tower, nicknamed Mechazilla. Elon Musk later posted a video on X inspecting the captured craft. During Friday’s flight, SpaceX also released another 26 of its newest Starlink v3 satellites; CNBC said the constellation already included more than 11,000 satellites in low Earth orbit, and that one Starship can carry up to 60 Starlink v3 satellites. The Hawaii Department of Transportation issued a temporary pause on some West Coast flights Friday because of debris risk, and the FAA separately issued a rare nationwide ground stop because of telecommunications system failures; both orders have since been lifted. No unverified after-hours share-price move is included here.

Reuters: Anthropic’s IPO was about six times oversubscribed by Thursday, with investors split over 2028 revenue forecasts and how durable its lead is

An investor who had placed an order said some buy-side forecasts were running 10% to 15% higher for 2026 and 2027, while an investor who did due diligence but decided not to participate was about 30% below the sell-side 2028 estimate.

The CFTC sued Louisiana on Friday to block a ban on regulated prediction markets; the state attorney general criticized Chair Selig on Saturday

The Block reported on Saturday that the CFTC and the United States filed a complaint on Friday in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, seeking to stop the state from enforcing a judge’s order that 31 entities, including Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase and Robinhood, stop offering event contracts in the state. The ex parte order requires the companies to geofence Louisiana, and a hearing on the state’s request for a preliminary injunction is set for November 30. In a November 13 statement, the CFTC quoted Chairman Michael Selig as saying event contracts have traded openly in Louisiana for years in compliance with the Commodity Exchange Act and CFTC rules, and he called the state’s “emergency” lawsuit “naked lawfare” and a cynical political stunt; the agency said Louisiana was the 12th state it has sued to defend exclusive federal jurisdiction over derivatives markets. Louisiana Attorney General Liz Murrill replied on X on Saturday, calling Selig the “obsequious standard bearer” for Kalshi and prediction markets and saying gambling has been regulated by states for 250 years. A Kalshi spokesperson told The Block that, under the state court’s logic, interest-rate swaps and weather derivatives would also be illegal gambling products; a Polymarket spokesperson said prediction markets are regulated under a federal framework, not a patchwork of state rules.
