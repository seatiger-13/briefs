4. OpenAI published a November 19 post describing the Performance Factory, an autonomous cloud system that uses Codex and the company’s models to find and fix performance issues and help keep ChatGPT fast. Its first workflow, the Regression Workflow, opens a task when a performance alert fires; an agent investigates the slowdown, records its findings and proposes a fix for an engineer to review, and after deployment another agent checks whether performance has returned to baseline. OpenAI said that in just over three months the workflow tracked more than 1,700 investigations and root-caused more than 600 cases, or about 17 automated investigations a day. The company said the bottleneck today may be waiting for an engineer to review and merge the fix, and that it is already exploring whether agents can do that themselves.

OpenAI 公布以 Codex 驅動的 Performance Factory，稱其自動調查及修補效能回歸，三個多月處理逾 1,700 宗調查並找出逾 600 宗根因

OpenAI 於 11 月 19 日撰文，介紹名為 Performance Factory 的自主系統，利用 Codex 及公司模型，在雲端自動發現並修補效能問題，以維持 ChatGPT 的速度。

6. Google said on November 19 that Tool Retrieval is now in preview in the Gemini Interactions API. The company said that when an agent is connected to dozens or even hundreds of tools, sending every full parameter schema on every request inflates input tokens, adds latency and can lower tool-selection accuracy. The new feature keeps the full library on standby and loads parameter schemas only when the current task needs them, while the model still sees the name and description of every deferred tool. Developers add a tool_search tool and set defer_loading: true on tools they want to hold back; the search runs on the server, and the feature also works with remote MCP servers and prefix caching. Google said there is no separate charge and that Tool Retrieval works with Gemini 3.6 and later models, including all Gemini 4 models, in the Interactions API.

Google 在 Gemini API 預覽推出 Tool Retrieval，讓代理按需要載入工具參數結構，以降低大型工具庫的輸入 token 並維持選工具準確度

Google 於 11 月 19 日宣布，Tool Retrieval 已在 Gemini Interactions API 以預覽形式推出。

官方解釋，當代理連接數十甚至數百種工具時，每次請求都發送完整參數結構會增加輸入 token、延遲，並可能降低選工具準確度；新功能讓代理只在當前任務需要時載入參數結構，模型仍會看到每個延後載入工具的名稱及描述。

開發者可加入 tool_search 工具，並對希望暫緩載入的工具設定 defer_loading: true；搜尋在伺服器端完成，亦可與遠端 MCP 伺服器及前綴快取一併使用。

Google 稱此功能沒有獨立收費，適用於 Gemini 3.6 及之後的模型（包括所有 Gemini 4 模型）。

https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-api-tool-retrieval/

The Monetary Authority of Singapore said on November 19 that on November 6 it had issued S$100 million of four-week tokenised MAS bills natively on its Singapore Dollar Test Network — to be renamed SG Digital Ledger — and settled the sale in wholesale central-bank digital currency, avoiding the reconciliation and intermediaries required when instruments are issued conventionally and tokenised later. Five primary dealers took part: DBS, HSBC’s Singapore branch, OCBC, Standard Chartered Singapore and UOB. The auction was more than twice subscribed, and the bills mature on December 4. Managing Director Chia Der Jiun said MAS intends next to scale up issuance, bring on more financial institutions and test wider use of the bills as collateral, and that more firms can join live trials in 2027. CoinDesk put the issue size at about $77.5 million.

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission said on November 19 that it had charged eight Colorado-based entities, likely operated by individuals overseas, with defrauding U.S. investors out of more than $37 million. According to the complaint, filed in federal court in Colorado, the defendants lured investors through social-media advertisements into group and private chats they controlled, first advised them to trade stocks on U.S. markets, then induced them to send as much money as possible to a supposed crypto-asset trading platform, leading victims to believe they were buying real crypto assets there. The SEC said the defendants used fake SEC certificates and filings with the SEC, FINRA or FinCEN to feign legitimacy, and that the scheme ran from August 2024 through May 2026 in four adviser-platform pairs, with the perpetrators abandoning one pair for the next after a few months. The agency is seeking permanent injunctions, disgorgement, prejudgment interest and civil penalties, and thanked authorities including the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission, the Monetary Authority of Singapore, the China Securities Regulatory Commission and Switzerland’s FINMA for assistance.

Tether said on November 19 that it had made a strategic investment in SCRYPT, a Swiss stablecoin trading and liquidity provider, to support the firm’s expansion from its existing liquidity, foreign-exchange and cross-border settlement infrastructure into third-party payments, and to make stablecoins easier for banks, brokers, fintechs and payment firms to use. The announcement did not disclose the size of the investment. SCRYPT describes itself as offering payments, trading, custody and asset management across fiat, stablecoins and digital assets, with segregated, MPC-secured custody in Switzerland. Tether chief executive Paolo Ardoino said a growing share of global finance already settles on stablecoins and that institutions need to use them inside the systems they already run; SCRYPT founder and chief executive Norman Wooding said the company would expand into fiat accounts and payments with Tether’s backing. This item records the disclosed institutional-adoption move; the investment amount was not published, so it is labeled unavailable and is not estimated.
